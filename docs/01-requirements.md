# 01 — Requirements

## Functional

FR-01 Create/manage incidents.
FR-02 Ingest heterogeneous operational events.
FR-03 Deduplicate and normalize evidence.
FR-04 Correlate related events.
FR-05 Generate evidence-backed hypotheses.
FR-06 Request additional evidence when confidence is insufficient.
FR-07 Recommend typed remediation actions.
FR-08 Enforce RBAC and policy checks.
FR-09 Require human approval for configured risk levels.
FR-10 Execute idempotent remediation.
FR-11 Record immutable audit events.
FR-12 Generate post-incident reports.

## Non-functional

NFR-01 Secure by default.
NFR-02 Horizontally scalable stateless API.
NFR-03 Observable with traces, metrics and structured logs.
NFR-04 Recoverable from worker/downstream failures.
NFR-05 Testable at domain, API, integration and AI-evaluation levels.
NFR-06 Reproducible model behavior through versioned prompts/evals.
